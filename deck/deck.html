Doorstub!

Tickets that refund themselves.

The organiser gets paid when you walk in. Otherwise your BCH comes back after the show.

Minus network fees and any fee listed on the show page.

The Story of Doorstub

When Bitcoin Cash forked in 2017, I was a miner. Later I helped promote BCH in Taiwan.

I think more people will book if no-shows get their BCH back.

I'm showing a 12-minute film about Doorstub online on 24 and 28 Nov, 13:00 UTC. The tickets are Doorstub tickets.

Benefits of Doorstub

  • Can't go? Hand the ticket back for your BCH.
  • Unused tickets refund after the show to the address that bought them.
  • Anyone can send that refund without a key, and my keeper bot does.
  • I tested signing with Selene, Paytaca and Cashonize.
  • npm test runs 143 attack tests, and libauth's 2026 BCH VM rejects every one.

BCH Usage

Your ticket is a CashTokens NFT. The money sits in its own UTXO at the show's Escrow contract.

Only checkIn pays the organiser the ticket price. It needs your signature: line 120 below requires the ticket input to sit at your own address.
